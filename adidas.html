Adidas US Sales Analysis

A retail performance analysis of US sales, built to find what drives profit and where regions and channels fall short.

Dataset9,648 transactions
Period2020 to 2021
ToolsPower BI, Power Query, DAX
StatusCompleted

View on GitHub ↗

[01]Overview

An end-to-end Power BI project on Adidas US sales across 6 retailers, 5 regions and 3 sales methods. The aim: uncover profitability drivers, regional performance gaps and sales channel efficiency to support business decisions.

[02]Business questions
  • Where do sales and profit come from by region, retailer, product and channel?
  • How did sales change from 2020 to 2021?
  • Which channel earns the best margin, and what does that say about investment?
  • Which retailers and products lead on profitability?
[03]Data preparation

Data cleaned, validated and transformed in Power Query, then modeled with DAX measures for KPIs. The source dataset is an Excel file included in the repository.

[05]Key findings
  • Recovery: sales dropped in 2020 and grew 294.23% year over year in 2021.
  • Region: the West leads with about $270M in sales.
  • Channel: Online has the highest profit margin, about 45%, despite the lowest sales volume.
  • Product: Men's Street Footwear leads in both sales and profitability.
  • Retailer: West Gear is the top retailer by profit and outperforms Walmart and Amazon on profitability; Walmart and Amazon fall below the $149.98M sales target.
[06]Business interpretation and recommendations
  • Digital expansion: a high online margin suggests more investment in that channel could lift overall margin.
  • Inventory focus: prioritize Men's Street Footwear production and marketing.
  • Regional strategy: re-evaluate Midwest marketing to close the performance gap.
