[01]Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard that turns raw retail data into business insights: revenue leadership, pricing, product assortment, and the effect of seasonality and promotions.

[02]Business questions
  • Which section drives revenue?
  • How do price and sales volume relate?
  • How do promotions and seasonality affect sales?
[03]Key KPIs

As shown on the dashboard.

[04]Key findings
  • Revenue leadership: the Man section contributes $35M, about 91.55% of total sales.
  • Pricing: a scatter plot compares unit price against sales volume.
  • Assortment: 251 unique products analyzed, with seasonal and promotional effects reviewed.
[05]Business interpretation

Revenue is heavily concentrated in one section, which makes the assortment sensitive to that section's performance. Detailed recommendations are not yet documented for this project.
