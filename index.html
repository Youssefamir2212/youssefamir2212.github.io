[02]About

I ask the question before I build the chart.

I work with SQL, Power BI, Excel and Python. My projects cover retail, fashion and sales data: cleaning it, modelling it, building dashboards and turning the numbers into recommendations.

My direction is Data Analyst, growing toward BI work over time. I do not want to be the person who only makes dashboards. I want to understand the business question, explore the data, validate it, analyze performance and explain what the numbers mean for a decision.
